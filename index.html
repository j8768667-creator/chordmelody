Chord & Melody Composer

Generate a singable melody from chords, or harmonise a melody with musically compatible chords. Runs entirely in your browser.
Self-contained • MIDI & WAV import
MIDI is analysed locally. In Chords mode, simultaneous notes are converted into chord symbols; in Melody mode, the clearest melodic track is imported.

Song reference and artist influence

Song search uses MusicBrainz metadata. It creates a new composition from broad genre, tempo and arranging traits; it does not copy a copyrighted recording or imitate an artist exactly. A licensed provider JSON file can supply authorised chord and melody data.
WAV analysis is local and best with clean audio. Melody extraction expects one clear lead line; chord extraction prefers sustained harmony; drum analysis estimates tempo and groove before generating matching material.
Separate chords with spaces, commas or bars. A bar line fixes grouping. Examples: C Am F G, Dm7 | G7 | Cmaj7 | Am7. Slash chords are accepted.
Use NOTE:DURATION, where duration is in beats. Example: C4:1 D4:0.5 E4:0.5 G4:2. Use R for a rest. Bar lines are optional.

Input guide and musical method
The generator scores notes using chord tones, scale membership, stepwise movement, rhythmic position and phrase contour. Harmonisation scores candidate diatonic chords against notes sounding in each bar and adds voice-leading preferences. Material is repeated and varied to reach the requested bar count. It is a deterministic music-theory engine, not a bundled copy of any third-party dataset or a trained commercial model.
