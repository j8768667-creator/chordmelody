Input guide and musical method
The generator scores notes using chord tones, scale membership, stepwise movement, rhythmic position and phrase contour. Harmonisation scores candidate diatonic chords against notes sounding in each bar and adds voice-leading preferences. Material is repeated and varied to reach the requested bar count. It is a deterministic music-theory engine, not a bundled copy of any third-party dataset or a trained commercial model.
