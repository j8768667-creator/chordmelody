Input guide and musical method
Alpha 3 plans a song as sections → phrases → motifs → variations → cadence. A one-bar motif is generated in scale-degree space, then developed as A A′ B A″ across each phrase, with contour and climax targets per phrase. Pitches are chosen with voice-leading preferences (repeated notes, seconds and thirds first, occasional fourths, few leaps beyond a sixth), snapped to chord tones on strong beats and long notes, and resolved through a cadence engine supporting perfect, plagal, imperfect and interrupted endings. Harmonisation scores diatonic candidates against sounding notes and adds cadential weighting at phrase boundaries. A musicality report scores harmony, melody, voice leading, rhythm, cadence, phrase quality and originality, and the weakest phrase can be regenerated on its own. It is a deterministic music-theory engine, not a bundled copy of any third-party dataset or a trained commercial model.
